Bochi Support

Bochi is a personal productivity app for tracking tasks, recurring tasks, rewards, timers, tags, and point activity.

Contact

For app issues, account help, feedback, or feature requests, email contact@bochi.app.

Privacy

Read the Bochi Privacy Policy for details about data collection, account data, diagnostics, and support requests.

Account deletion

Signed-in users can delete their Bochi account in the app from Settings. Account deletion removes the Bochi account and synced account data from active backend systems. This cannot be undone.

Deleting your Bochi account does not cancel billing managed by Apple. Manage Apple subscriptions at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.
