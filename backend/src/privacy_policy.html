Bochi Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 10, 2026

Bochi is a personal productivity app for tracking one-off tasks, recurring tasks, rewards, timers, tags, and point activity. This policy explains what information Bochi collects, why it is used, and the choices you have.

Short version

  • Bochi uses your data to provide app functionality: sign-in, sync, premium access, reliability, security, and support.
  • Bochi does not sell your personal information, does not show third-party ads, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • The App Store privacy label declares Crash Data, Performance Data, and Other Diagnostic Data as collected for App Functionality and linked to the user's identity. That diagnostic data is described below.
  • You can delete your Bochi account in the app from Settings when you are signed in.

Information you provide or create

Bochi may collect and store the following information when you use the app:

  • Account information, including your Sign in with Apple app-specific user identifier, your Apple-provided email address if Apple provides it and you choose to share it, and session tokens used to keep you signed in.
  • App content you create, including task names and descriptions, recurring task names and descriptions, reward names and descriptions, timers and timer intervals, tags, dependencies, point balances, and point activity such as task completions, reward purchases, refunds, vault deposits, and vault interest.
  • App settings, including theme choices, list preferences, premium access state, and sync state.
  • Purchase-related entitlement information, including Apple subscription or purchase status, product identifier, expiry date where applicable, and Apple original transaction identifier. Apple processes payment details; Bochi does not receive or store your payment card information.

Reminder schedules and local notification settings are stored on your device. Bochi uses them to schedule notifications through iOS.

Some Bochi fields are free-form text. If you type sensitive information into task, reward, timer, or tag fields, that information may be stored and synced as part of your app content.

Diagnostic information

Bochi uses, or intends to use, Sentry for crash reporting and app reliability diagnostics. Diagnostic events may include crash logs, stack traces, error messages, app version, operating system version, device model, performance measurements, hang information, technical breadcrumbs, sanitized request URLs, timestamps, and related runtime context.

Diagnostic information may be linked to your identity, such as your Bochi account identifier, email address, session, or device context, so we can understand and fix issues affecting signed-in users. We use this information only for app functionality, reliability, debugging, abuse prevention, and security.

Sentry and Bochi's hosting providers may also receive technical network information, such as IP address, as part of receiving app requests and diagnostic events. Bochi does not use this information for advertising or tracking.

How Bochi uses information

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To sync your app content across your devices when you are signed in.
  • To calculate and display balances, task completions, reward purchases, lockouts, timers, and history.
  • To manage premium access and prevent one Apple entitlement from being linked to multiple accounts.
  • To schedule local reminders and notifications that you configure.
  • To maintain, debug, protect, and improve Bochi's reliability and performance.
  • To respond to support, legal, or safety requests.

Sharing and processors

Bochi shares information only with service providers needed to run the app and backend. These may include Apple for Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases, and device operating system services; Sentry for crash reporting and diagnostics; and infrastructure providers such as Amazon Web Services and Cloudflare for hosting, database, network delivery, security, and logging.

These providers process information on Bochi's behalf or under their own terms for the services they provide. Bochi does not sell your personal information and does not share it with advertising networks or data brokers.

Retention

Bochi keeps account information, synced app content, purchase entitlement records, and sync records for as long as needed to provide the app, maintain your account, prevent conflicts, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Deleted content may remain for a limited time in backups, logs, or sync tombstones before it is removed in the normal course of operations. Diagnostic data is kept only as long as needed for reliability, debugging, security, and operational records.

When you delete your account in the app, Bochi deletes your account record and synced account data from active backend systems. Some information may remain temporarily in backups, logs, diagnostic systems, or records Bochi is legally required or legitimately permitted to keep. Deleting your Bochi account does not cancel billing managed by Apple; Apple subscriptions must be managed separately through your Apple account.

Security

Bochi uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including HTTPS for network communication, secure session handling, passwordless Sign in with Apple, and restricted backend access. No internet service can guarantee perfect security.

Your choices

  • You can choose whether to sign in with Apple. Some sync and account features require sign-in.
  • You can choose what content to enter into Bochi.
  • You can manage notification permission in iOS Settings.
  • You can delete local app data by deleting the app from your device.
  • When signed in, you can delete your Bochi account from Settings in the app.
  • You can manage or cancel Apple subscriptions through your Apple account.
  • You can request access, correction, export, or additional deletion help by contacting Bochi.

Children and young users

Bochi's App Store age rating is 4+ in most countries and regions. The regional App Store ratings are All in Brazil, 00+ in Korea, and 00+ in Vietnam. These ratings describe content suitability and parental-control treatment in the App Store; they do not change the privacy practices described in this policy.

Bochi is a general-audience productivity app and is not specifically directed to children. If a child uses Bochi, a parent or guardian should review this policy and supervise use. Children should not create an account, use Sign in with Apple, make purchases, or sync data unless allowed by their parent or guardian and by their Apple account settings.

If you believe Bochi has collected personal information from a child without appropriate consent, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

International processing

Bochi is available globally through the App Store except in mainland China. Bochi and its service providers may process information in the United States, the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area, and other locations where they operate. Where required, Bochi relies on appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

Changes

Bochi may update this policy when the app, backend, service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when the policy changes materially.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Bochi at contact@bochi.app.
